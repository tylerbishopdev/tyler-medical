Overall Summary — Plasma-Cell / Monoclonal Screen:

Reassuring (argues against a plasma-cell dyscrasia):
  • SPEP shows a normal pattern with no monoclonal protein / no M-spike.
  • Total protein (6.5) and all globulin fractions are within range — no hypergammaglobulinemia.
  • Serum free kappa (10.6 mg/L) is normal.

What this does NOT fully exclude (honest limits):
  • A normal SPEP can miss a small monoclonal band — serum immunofixation (IFE) is the sensitive confirmatory test and was not part of this order (no reflex was triggered because there was no visible M-spike).
  • Only free kappa was measured. A low-level light-chain (AL amyloidosis / light-chain MGUS) process is best excluded with free lambda + the kappa/lambda ratio.
  • No urine studies (UPEP / urine immunofixation) were done.

To completely close this workup: add serum free lambda with kappa/lambda ratio, serum immunofixation, and a 24-hour urine with immunofixation. Quantitative immunoglobulins (IgG/IgA/IgM) would further characterize the low-normal gamma fraction.
